2. Financial Deductions & Fee Matrix ｜ 金融結算扣款與補償機制

All calculations follow strict compliance rules to cover processing fees. If an item includes a customized producer loss fee, that fee is deducted first from the store credit voucher pool. Any remaining fee balance is then deducted from the cash refund pool.
本站退款依循金融合規標準計算。若訂單內含客製化商品損耗費，系統將優先從會員代金券額度中扣除；若代金券餘額不足折抵，差額將自動轉由現金退款池中加計扣除。

5% Gateway Administrative Fee ｜ 5% 金融管道手續費

Applies **exclusively to the cash/gateway** segment of a refund to cover third-party processing and manual audit overhead. Vouchers are returned at 100% value.
僅針對「現金/刷卡原路退回」部分收取，用以充抵第三方支付網關手續費及跨境人工審核開支。若選擇全額轉為儲值金則完全免除此費用。

Producer Cancellation Loss Fee ｜ 客製化生產損耗補償

Applies if a product has an active custom manufacturing fee percentage set. Calculated dynamically as: `Item Price × Cancellation Fee %`.
若選購的藝術品包含工坊生產階段鎖定標籤，將依據產品設定之百分比，由系統自乘計算 `單品單價 × 取消手續費率` 後予以扣除。

Guest Payout Conversion ｜ 訪客購物金轉化

Non-Member Store Credit Claim Rules
匿名訪客購物金申領與帳戶轉換機制

When a casual guest buyer requests a refund via store credits, our system dispatches an encrypted registration link to their verified checkout email.
當非註冊會員（訪客）選擇以「100%全額購物金」形式退款時，系統將向其結算信箱自動發送一條高強度加密的註冊帳戶連結。訪客點擊該專屬連結完成新會員註冊後，原先孤立的退款面值將在 1 秒內自動激活並掛載至新錢包中，享 1 年（365天）全功能消費折抵。

How do Hong Kong bank refunds process?
香港公司線下銀行轉帳退款如何運作？

If your original checkout was processed via Hong Kong Bank Transfer, our first-stage automated email will securely collect your clearing identifiers (including Faster Payment System (FPS) tokens or international SWIFT bank details). Manual offline clearing is then executed smoothly by our accounting staff team.

若您原先使用香港銀行轉帳等線下通路，系統首階段信件將向您收取受款明細（如轉數快識別碼或跨境電匯代碼），隨後由我們駐港財務人員手動匯回。

What happens to a gifted voucher?
如果我取消的是送給朋友的電子禮券怎麼辦？

Upon confirming the cancellation of an unused voucher product, our platform automatically revokes the original token key string. Simultaneously, an alert is sent to the recipient's email to notify them that the gift link has been deactivated, routing them safely to a standard landing notice page if clicked.

全新未動用的送禮類電子券一經取消，原券碼將即刻失效。系統亦會同步向收件信箱發送通知，若對方不慎點擊原禮物連結，將被流暢引導至禮券已失效之提示頁面。
